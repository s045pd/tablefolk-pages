Help & support

Updated: September 29, 2026

Try the steps below first. If the problem remains, email the Tablefolk developer.

Contact support

Support email: s045pd.x@gmail.com

Email support

No GitHub account is required. Describe the issue and include your app version, iPhone model, iOS version and steps to reproduce it. A guide or prompt name or ID is enough to identify content. Do not send passwords, payment details or complete receipts, and redact screenshots first. Emails are not public feedback; see our privacy policy for data handling. We do not promise a fixed response time.

Optional: public feedback

Send feedback on GitHub

A GitHub account is required. The Tablefolk developer maintaining this website reviews feedback. Describe the issue and include your app version, iPhone model, iOS version and steps to reproduce it. For a particular guide or prompt, its name or ID is enough.

GitHub feedback is public, not a private support ticket. Do not post passwords, Apple Account details, complete receipts, payment information, personal answers or private conversations. Redact screenshots first. Use the support email above for personal information or privacy-rights requests.

I purchased access, but paid content is still locked

  1. Check that the App Store is using the Apple Account used for the purchase, and connect to the internet.
  2. Open Tablefolk Settings, choose “Restore purchases”, and wait for the result.
  3. If Apple shows a pending transaction, wait for confirmation before checking again. Do not repeatedly purchase the same access.
  4. If the problem remains, email support with the displayed status and steps, without sharing a complete receipt.

A purchase unlocks content but does not enable adult topics. Adult users must choose “Spicy questions” separately in Settings.

Cancel a subscription or request a refund

If you purchased a subscription, manage it in iPhone Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. Deleting the app or resetting local data does not cancel a subscription. Buying lifetime access does not automatically cancel an existing subscription either.

See Apple’s instructions for cancelling subscriptions. Apple handles refunds; follow its refund instructions. Available purchase options are shown in the app.

Delete local data

Open Tablefolk Settings → Reset local data. This clears local favorites, age confirmation, language and walkthrough preferences, and turns adult topics off. Valid purchases are kept; manage purchase records and subscriptions through Apple. System backups may retain older information and must be managed separately in device settings.

Offline access, language and prompt order

Guides and prompts are bundled for offline browsing. Purchases, restoration and these web pages require an internet connection. Switch between Chinese and English in Settings → Language. Prompt banks open in a new random order, with saved prompts first.

Suggest a content or translation correction

If wording sounds unnatural, rules are unclear or content seems unsuitable, provide the guide or prompt name or ID, current language and suggested change. Do not upload the complete paid question bank. Anyone can pass on a question without answering or explaining.

Policies and terms

The privacy policy explains local information, Apple purchases and GitHub traffic. The terms of use explain participation and paid access.
