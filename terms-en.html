1. Games and participation

Tablefolk provides guides, instructional demos and conversation prompts for adults playing together in person. It does not offer online matchmaking or private messaging. Rules can differ by region or household; agree on your version before playing.

Beverage-based activities in this app are intended as water games. “Take a sip” or “take a cup” is a game-counting convention, not a requirement to drink alcohol, finish a cup quickly or consume large quantities of liquid. Use points, passes or another agreed alternative instead. No beverage consumption is required.

Anyone may pass, decline to participate or stop without explaining. Adult topics are off by default and should only be enabled when everyone participating is an adult and comfortable. Do not use prompts to require physical contact, sexual acts, disclosure of private records, harassment or humiliation.
