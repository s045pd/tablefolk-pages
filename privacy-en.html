Privacy policy

Effective and updated: September 29, 2026

This policy is provided by the Tablefolk app developer. It covers the Tablefolk iPhone app, this information website, and support feedback. The public website and support repository are maintained through the GitHub account s045pd.

In short: Tablefolk does not require an account, record your answers, or include advertising or behavioral analytics SDKs. Favorites and preferences stay on your device. Apple handles purchases. GitHub processes website traffic and any feedback you choose to submit under its own policies.

1. Information stored on your device

  • IDs of saved guides and prompts, used to display favorites and put them first.
  • Your language, walkthrough speed and adult-age confirmation, used to remember settings. Age confirmation records only whether you confirmed, not a date of birth or identity document.
  • Your adult-topic choice for the current session. It is off by default and is not enabled by purchasing access.

This information is kept in local system preferences and is not uploaded to a Tablefolk server. We do not store your answers, conversations or recordings. The app does not request access to contacts, precise location, photos, the camera or microphone.

2. Purchases and restoration

Purchases and restoration use Apple’s App Store and StoreKit. On your device, the app processes product information and Apple-verified transaction information, such as product and transaction IDs, purchase type, expiration dates where applicable, and refund or revocation status. This determines whether paid content is available. These records are not sent to our own server.

We do not receive your payment-card details, Apple Account password or payment verification information. Apple handles payments, accounts and store services under its privacy policy. System backups, diagnostics and store records also depend on Apple’s policies and your device settings; these are separate from the app’s local favorites and answers.

3. This website and GitHub feedback

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It includes no additional analytics, advertising scripts, third-party fonts or tracking cookies. GitHub’s Pages documentation states that GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, even when visitors are not signed in.

Our feedback link uses GitHub Issues and requires a GitHub account. Your username, text and attachments are visible in a public repository to the Tablefolk developer and other visitors. We use submitted information to investigate problems, respond and maintain the product, not for advertising profiles.

Do not post passwords, Apple Account details, complete receipts, payment information, personal answers, private conversations or unredacted screenshots. GitHub processes visits and feedback under its privacy statement and may process or store data outside your region. We do not control its infrastructure or platform retention periods.

4. Retention and deletion

Local favorites and preferences remain until you remove favorites, change settings, reset local data or uninstall the app. Choose “Reset local data” in the app’s Settings to clear favorites, age confirmation, language and walkthrough preferences. An Apple backup or device restore may bring back older information; manage backups through Apple’s system settings.

A reset does not delete Apple’s purchase records, cancel a subscription or remove a valid purchase. Manage subscriptions, if offered, separately in your Apple Account settings.

Public feedback remains available for issue handling and history until edited or deleted by its author or a repository maintainer, or handled under GitHub’s rules. To request help correcting or deleting your feedback, contact the maintainer through the support page and identify the issue link without reposting sensitive details. GitHub controls deletion from its own logs, caches and backups under its policies.

5. Your choices and rights

You can review or remove favorites, change preferences, turn adult topics off, avoid submitting feedback, and choose not to open external websites. Tracking permission is not required for local content. A purchase does not constitute consent to adult topics.

Where applicable law provides access, correction, deletion or other personal-information rights, you can submit a request through support. We cannot remotely read data that exists only on your device; use the app’s local controls for that information. For data independently processed by Apple or GitHub, use that platform’s privacy-rights process.

6. Adult content and policy changes

Tablefolk is intended for adults. Adult topics require a separate opt-in. We do not design features to collect children’s information. If you believe public feedback contains a child’s personal information, contact the maintainer for help.

If the app’s data handling changes, we will update this policy and its date, and provide additional notice or obtain consent where required by law.

7. Contact

Contact the Tablefolk developer through the GitHub feedback link on our Help & support page. Email and private support tickets are not currently available. Public feedback is not suitable for sensitive personal information.
