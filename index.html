At your table

朋友到齐了，一起玩吧。

Tablefolk 是聚会玩法与聊天题库 App。挑一个玩法，看懂规则，再和在场的朋友一起玩。

中英双语内容，可离线浏览。收藏喜欢的玩法与题目，按你们的尺度选择话题。成人话题默认关闭，购买不会自动开启。

这是 Tablefolk 的公开信息网站，不是 App 下载或购买页面。是否已上架、可用方案与价格，以 App Store 和 App 内实际显示为准。

Everyone is here. What should you play?

Tablefolk brings party-game guides and bilingual conversation prompts into one place. Read the rules, save your favorites, and play with the people around you. Content is available offline. Adult topics are off by default and require a separate choice to enable.

This is the app’s public information site, not a download or checkout page. Availability, purchase options and prices are shown in the App Store and the app.
